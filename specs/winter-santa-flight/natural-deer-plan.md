## Prepared source and asset compilation

Source: `https://grab3d.com/dl/deer/deer-light.glb`, publisher page `https://grab3d.com/models/animals/deer/`, CC0 dedication from `https://grab3d.com/dl/deer/LICENSE.txt`. Original SHA-256: `E1C790CF50F1CBAD11FDF945E4A7E0D40580A35EF17E4644542A77449C745023`. The research copy has one indexed textured mesh, no skin/animation, 372,733 triangles, POSITION/NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0, and two embedded JPEG images. Its node transform is identity. The live side-view render established that the head faces source +Z.

The preparation script accepts a local original GLB input, validates its hash/header/accessor bounds and writes a self-contained standard glTF 2.0 binary. Do not automatically fetch or execute anything from the model. Make it reproducible using a checked-in script, pinned optimizer and source/license/hash notes. CC0 attribution is optional, but preserve the publisher's public-domain dedication and source URL; do not misattribute the model as original portfolio geometry.

1. Use MeshoptSimplifier.simplifyWithAttributes with source normals/UVs (stride 5, weights [.1,.1,.1,.5,.5]), target 60,000 indices, error .005 and Prune. The inspected trial produced 19,999 triangles at relative error .002012. Compact/reindex used vertices. Preserve normals and UV seams.
2. Bake canonical coordinates `x = sourceZ * 2.2 + .2`, `y = sourceY * 2.2 - .88`, `z = -sourceX * 2.2` (+90-degree Y rotation and uniform scale); rotate normals accordingly. Keep original paired body origins 0/4 and lateral offsets +/-.65. This fits the old muzzle and hoof heights and gives adequate sleigh/pair gaps without changing spacing API or trajectories.
3. Resize the base-color image to at most 1024px and encode WebP near quality 85 using Sharp. Omit the source metallic/roughness image; use metallicFactor 0 and roughness near .96. Embed the image with standard EXT_texture_webp and linear/mipmapped sampler. Keep float positions/normals/UVs, unsigned-short indices where safe, unsigned-byte joint indices and float skin weights. Further custom compression is unnecessary if the GLB is under 1.5 MB.
4. Bake a nine-bone skin: root plus upper/lower for the four limbs. Bone names must remain `deer-leg-1-upper/lower` through 4 so existing keyed animation can drive them. Rest hip positions (canonical) are front `[.55,.65,+/-.25]`, rear `[-.8,.78,+/-.25]`; knee/hock positions front `[.52,-.25,+/-.25]`, rear `[-1.22,-.25,+/-.25]`. Source leg cross-sections confirmed front hooves around source Z=.15 and rear around -.65. Use these absolute rest points to derive local child offsets and inverse bind translation matrices. Root at identity; one SkinnedMesh contains the full textured deer and skeleton.
5. Compute smooth, normalized skin weights: select front/rear by canonical X around -.35 and side by Z sign. Use vertical fade `fade = 1 - smooth(y,.3,rear ? .8 : .75)`, anatomical longitudinal mask `rear ? 1-smooth(x,-.85,-.45) : smooth(x,.28,.48)`, then `influence = fade * longitudinal`. These continuous masks retain full lower-leg influence at actual front/rear limb locations while rooting the inner abdomen/groin; actual renders exposed belly distortion with the earlier Gaussian gate. Do not gate by lateral distance: the actual inner shin surface approaches the centerline and must remain fully leg weighted. Blend upper/lower influence around knee Y=-.25, approximately [-.37,-.09]. Use root + selected upper/lower weights, sum 1, other slot 0. Keep torso/head/rack rooted and avoid abrupt shoulder seams.
6. Add eight named non-rendering antler attachment nodes. For each rack side, choose four high surface points above canonical Y~2.1, spaced at least .30–.35 raw units apart, favoring high tips. Offset slightly along the source normal. These are actual new-rack attachments, not the old antler coordinates. Asset geometry/node naming should be meaningful. No generated-code authorship annotations or local paths in shipped metadata.
